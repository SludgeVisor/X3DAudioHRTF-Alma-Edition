## IRCAM LISTEN HRTF Database

**Applies to:** all included `*.mhr` HRTF data files (derived from subject
`IRC_1037` of the IRCAM LISTEN HRTF Database)

The included HRTF data was recorded in the IRCAM anechoic room
(1 place Igor Stravinsky, 75004 Paris, France) in 2002–2003 by the Room
Acoustics Team of IRCAM (Institut de Recherche et Coordination
Acoustique/Musique), principal investigator Olivier Warusfel.

The original recordings are distributed in IRCAM native and SOFA formats.
The files in this repository were obtained from the IRCAM LISTEN database and
converted to the `.mhr` format used by this mod. The original, unmodified
data is available at:

- Original project page (canonical citation URL; no longer online):
  http://recherche.ircam.fr/equipes/salles/listen/
- SOFA-format mirror (includes the `IRC_1037_*` subject files):
  https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/listen (hrtf)
- Catalogue entry (SONICOM Ecosystem, Database #78; lists the dataset as
  CC BY 4.0 Attribution):
  https://ecosystem.sonicom.eu/databases/78
